## Sales Pipeline

The sheet provides a comprehensive sales performance overview, tracking 499 total deals with metrics on revenue, win rates, deal closure timelines, and loss analysis across customer segments and products.

* Out of 3,000 tickets, 50 are critical and 59 remain open, while 81 at-risk accounts have been identified requiring intervention.

* Average resolution time is 58.2 hours with minimal variation across priority levels—low (58.92), medium (57.49), and high (57.62)—suggesting priority level does not significantly impact resolution speed.

* Authentication leads in high-severity tickets (156 of 450), signaling critical reliability concerns despite Notifications (601) having the highest overall support load.
